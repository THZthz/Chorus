**Why Radiance and Hush.** Two principles must put all four endings in play and
cross every subsystem. Radiance is CS's Lantern role: it carries the desire
ladder (Dream + WITS → the Promise; the ascension rites) *and* spawns RAPTURE
(the 30% fascinating induction — the visions death). Hush is CS's Winter role:
grim lore spawns DREAD on the same 30% (the despair death) and its still airs
are the summon's second pillar. The summon needs radiance ≥6 **and** hush ≥2
**and** ways ≥2 (CS's `spirit_heartc_edge {knock:2, heart:6, edge:2}`, knock
role renamed), so no subsystem can be won without touching both principles.

## 2. The scale

**1 CS second → 1 engine minute.** CS's 60 s standard action is the 1 h verb
duration; the 60 s season cycle is the 1 h WAIT window; the summon's 180 s is
the guest's 3 h `expires`; affliction's 180 s is the fever's 3 h (then frailty);
the influence hops' 60 s are 1 h `expires` clocks (set at spawn, authored
`on expire` REPLACE chains, §9.32).

**Lore magnitudes 2 / 6 / 18 — each rung triples, the ladder shortens.** CS's
final-rite rule is a magnitude-36 pool (`minorlanternvictory_trigger
{lantern:36}`, dossier §3.4); the slice's victory pool is **radiance ≥ 24**,
closing exactly one way: **18 + 6 — the top two rungs pooled** in the 3-card
invocation. The verified door path pays it at seed 1837: the BELLWOOD
permutation deals THE_LOST_BELL (radiance 18) on draw 1 and THE_BELL_HEART
(radiance 6) by draw 6. (An 18 + 2 + 2 + 2 arm would need four invocation
cards in a 0..3 slot — impossible; deleted.) Ascension thresholds rise
12 → 18 → 24 across the three marks (CS's marks 3→6 with rising demand); the
desire card is born at marks 3, exact.

**Chances kept CS-exact:** the 1→30 / 5→70 / 10→90 cult-business convention
(one ROLL(1,10) tiered on the pooled principle — the 90/70/30 convention's
non-expedition home); the 70% suspicion hunt; summon losing-control 30% →
regain-mastery 70%; ascension failure 10% (the Grand-rite rate); despair/visions
death at 3 processed marks (`inducesdespair:3` / `fascination:3`, exact);
begging's 30%-of-2-clean; starvation eats one VIGOR, none left is death. Three
further 30% rolls are the slice's own borrowings, not CS numbers — flagged in
§6 (the employment drain; the lore-read inductions; the fever/benediction pair).

Aspect vocabulary (small, closed): `vigor wits yearning weary dim pale
radiance hush ways ritual desire vow promise marks toll fever frail dread
rapture ease mending bell_voice candle salve cost book post hale unmet believer
disciple wood site.*` plus the two emitter aspects `labor job` (recipe-declared
only, §9.48). Flags are flag-aspects (`sold`) — aspects double as the only
script-readable numbers (`object.aspect.cost`).

**Two engine laws the whole plan obeys (both verified by run, 2026-10-15):**

1. **REPLACE's successor resolves strictly inside trigger bodies** — a
   never-alive prototype there rejects every commit on the verb ("unresolvable
   ref"), the brick the first skeleton shipped. Every card-borne transform in
   `on complete`/`on expire` bodies is therefore **DESTROY + SPAWN + SET**
   (the expired lifetime re-SET where the chain needs one). In recipe bodies
   REPLACE into prototypes is legal and used where it reads best
   (dream.dedication, work.post, season.sick, rite.fix_desire — the mint
   carries the prototype's aspects, verified); REPLACE into an *entity* name is
   refused everywhere ("no kind") — **the standard restore idiom for the three
   foundations is `DESTROY WITH` + `SPAWN resource:K` + `SET(K, aspect:k, 1)`**
   (kind-mints are aspect-less; the SET re-arms the slot query), verified
   closing the drain→restore→drain loop.
2. **Predicates read aspects and attrs, never names** — there is no
   `name:`/`kind:` term on a bound role (§6.3). Every card a recipe must
   recognize inside a bound role carries a one-level marker aspect; WAKEFULNESS
   and GLIMMER stay bare because nothing ever binds them.

Deck entries: `SEASONS` (14-slot weighted spec, `reset`), `MOODS` (the six
moods, `reset`), `BELLWOOD` (8 slots, **no reset, no defaultcard** — the wood
empties, then the draw fails loud, §9.42).

## 4. Recipe inventory — 102 recipes

Grouped by subsystem; ids are lowercase dotted, verb-prefixed (nightjar style).
Authoring conventions for every table below:

- "Roll ≤n" means `ROLL(1, 10), IF roll <= n -> (…)` — the ROLL+IF idiom
  (§9.41); the tiered convention decodes the same way (see talk.business_rad).
- **Silver is the attr form**: gains and drains are
  `SET(ACTORS, silver, ±n)`; gates read `actors.attr.silver >= …` (attr vs
  aspect path comparisons are legal, §6.3). There is no SILVER card.
- Whens that recognize a bound card do it by **marker aspect over a total**
  (`total(with, aspect:weary) >= 1`), with `count(with) = n` pinned where the
  recipe wants an exact hand — first-card paths would read whatever the player
  bound first.
- **Prose interpolation knows only the six role tokens and `{roll}`** (§5.3)
  — `{to.aspect.marks}` would render raw; narrate counts in words or via
  `{roll}`.
- Every counter change is a SET delta; every lifetime a spawn-time
  `SET …, expires` plus an `on expire` DESTROY+SPAWN chain (law 1); `aspects`
  marks the CS-xtrigger emitters (§9.48). **Total: 102** — the dossier's
  100–200 band at its floor by design; every recipe earns its place, and later
  phases may add gates but never padding.
